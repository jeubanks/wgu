Color scheme mockups for the study site

Each scheme shows the same sample page in light and dark. The numbers under each page are measured contrast ratios against that page's background. AA needs 4.5:1 for body text, and AAA needs 7:1.

"Bold vs body" measures how much bold text stands apart from regular text. In the current dark mode the two are the same color, so the ratio is 1.00 and bold relies on weight alone.
